1. Overview & Local-First Storage

MediSip: Pill & Water Reminder ("the App") is designed as an offline-first personal health and hydration reminder tool. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not transmit, store, or sell your personal medication schedules, hydration logs, or family profile entries on any external developer servers. All health and reminder records you enter are stored locally on your Android device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or medical history on our servers.
  • We do not share your medication names, dosages, or water intake records with third parties.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free for all users, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-medical device identifiers and usage data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Coarse location data (IP-based country/region for relevant ads)
  • App interaction and ad performance diagnostics (crash logs, ad impressions, and rewarded video completions)

You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads. For more information on how Google uses data, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Device Permissions Used

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used strictly on your device to alert you at your scheduled medication and hydration times.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used solely to load Google AdMob advertisements.

5. Data Backup & Deletion

Because all personal health logs reside locally on your device, you have 100% control over your data. You can delete individual entries, clear all app data from your Android Settings, or uninstall the App at any time to permanently erase all locally stored records.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and family caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
